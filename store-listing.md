## Long description

**Tab Hibernator Pro** is a lightweight, privacy-first tab manager that puts inactive tabs to sleep so your browser stays fast and your memory stays free.

Open a research rabbit hole, switch to a long video, or keep 50 tabs "just in case". Tab Hibernator Pro quietly suspends the ones you aren't using, and brings them back the instant you click on them. Nothing is closed, nothing is lost, nothing is sent to a server.

### What you get in v2.0.2

- **Memory pressure tiers.** Choose how aggressively the extension reacts to system RAM pressure. *Balanced* is the new default and works for most people. *Aggressive* trims thresholds when you have 20+ tabs. *Conservative* only kicks in during critical memory pressure.

- **Per-domain timeouts.** Some sites deserve a longer leash. Give `github.com` 4 hours so your long-running PRs don't get put to sleep. Set a domain to `0` to never hibernate it, no whitelist required.

- **Right-click "Pause hibernation."** Reading something you don't want to lose? Right-click the tab and pause hibernation for 15 minutes, 1 hour, until you close it, or forever. A small ⏸ badge appears on the toolbar so you can see the pause is active.

- **Toolbar preview.** Hover the extension icon to see what's sleeping, how much memory you've freed, and the titles of your most recently suspended tabs.

- **Quiet performance.** Tab activity, scroll position, and form input are now batched into a single storage write. The result is noticeably less background I/O churn when you have many tabs open.

### Always protected

- Audio and video playing tabs are never touched.
- Pinned tabs are never touched.
- Forms you're filling in are never touched.
- Whitelisted domains are never touched.
- A new tab you just opened gets a 5-minute grace period before it's eligible.

### Privacy first

- No analytics, no telemetry, no external servers.
- All settings, previews, and stats live on your device.
- Tab previews are off by default. If you turn them on, screenshots stay on this device only.
- Open source. You can read every line.

### Keyboard friendly

- `Ctrl/⌘ + Shift + K` opens the Quick-Wake HUD: a search palette to jump to any tab, sleeping or active.

Tab Hibernator Pro is free and built for people who care about keeping their browser fast. If it saved you memory or time, you can chip in to help fund the next update.
