    How stashing works

    Hibernating frees RAM but keeps tabs open. Stashing closes tabs and saves them here for later, so you can clear clutter without losing anything.

    In the toolbar popup

    Stash Tab

    Closes the tab you're on and saves it to this board, freeing its memory completely.

    Best for a single page you'll read later but don't want open.

    Stash Group

    Saves a whole Chrome tab group, including its name, color, and every tab. Restores exactly as it was.

    Best for a full project or research session in a tab group.
    Appears only when your active tab is in a group.

    Stash All

    Moves every already-hibernated tab here in one click, closing them to reclaim all the RAM.

    Best for a clean slate at the end of the day.
    1 Click a stash button in the popup
    2 Tabs close and land on this board
    3 Hit Restore below to reopen
    Hibernate

    Tab stays open, just frozen, and auto-wakes when clicked. Use it for tabs you'll return to soon.

    Stash

    Tab closes and is saved here to reopen manually. Use it to declutter for the long haul.
