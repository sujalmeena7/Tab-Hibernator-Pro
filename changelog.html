v2.0.1 Update

Stability & Privacy Update

This release squashes some important bugs and tightens privacy. Here's what changed since 2.0.

Waking Works After a Restart

Hibernated tabs that survived a browser restart could get stuck — the Wake this tab button did nothing. Tab Hibernator now re-links those tabs on startup and always keeps the original page handy, so they restore reliably every time.

Unsaved Forms Are Safer

A timing bug could let a tab with a half-filled form get hibernated while you were scrolling, wiping your input. Form detection now runs on its own track and can no longer be interrupted, so partially-filled forms stay protected.

Smarter Whitelist Matching

Whitelisted domains now match exactly and by sub-domain only. Previously a short entry could accidentally protect unrelated sites — and a look-alike domain could sneak past a real one. Your whitelist now does precisely what you'd expect.

Privacy-First Tab Previews

Screenshot previews on the hibernated screen are now opt-in and off by default — no page content is captured unless you turn it on under Settings. We also sharpened the low-memory detection so it kicks in correctly on high-RAM machines.
